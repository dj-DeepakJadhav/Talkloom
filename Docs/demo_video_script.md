# Talkloom: 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Video Script
**Target Hackathon:** Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (Best Apps & Agents Track)  
**Pitch Hook:** "Don't save it. Learn it. Speak it."  
**Duration:** Exactly 3:00 (180 seconds)

---

### Scene 1: The Problem — The Save Graveyard (0:00 – 0:20)
* **Visual:** Close-up of a smartphone screen scrolling through hundreds of saved bookmarks, bookmarked YouTube videos, saved Reddit threads, and downloaded PDF rental contracts that the user never actually opened again.
* **Voiceover (Warm, energetic, relatable):**  
  *"Every language learner has one: The Save Graveyard. A collection of 40-minute German podcasts, Instagram reels, and 12-page Berlin rental contracts you swore you'd study 'this weekend'. But saving isn't learning. And flashcards won't help when your Munich landlord is demanding your Kaution on the phone."*
* **Visual Transition:** Cuts to Talkloom logo glowing in warm brass gold against an obsidian broadsheet canvas.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Meet Talkloom: The audio-first precision instrument that turns any real-world content into instant speaking fluency."*

---

### Scene 2: Universal Ingestion Studio & Tavily Cultural Grounding (0:20 – 0:45)
* **Visual:** User opens Talkloom at `http://localhost:3000`. Clicks the elevated center `(+)` gold action pill.
* **On-Screen Action:** The Universal Ingestion Studio slides up with frosted obsidian glass (`blur(24px)`). 
  - Show the 3 segmented tabs: `[ 📷 Camera OCR | 📄 File Upload | 🔗 Web Link ]`.
  - User pastes an authentic German news article link on rental caps:  
    `https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/gesellschaft/wohnungsmarkt-mieten-100.html`.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"With the Universal Ingestion Studio, paste a YouTube link, drop a PDF lease, or snap a photo of a letter. Talkloom doesn't just read the words—it queries the Tavily Search API in real-time to ground the lesson in genuine cultural and statutory facts, like § 551 BGB German rental deposit caps."*

---

### Scene 3: Nebius Token Factory & NVIDIA Nemotron Lesson Compilation (0:45 – 1:10)
* **Visual:** Screen shows instantaneous compilation indicator with "Processing with Nemotron...".
* **On-Screen Action:** The compiled Lesson DSL surfaces seamlessly:
  - Vocabulary lemmas (`die Kaution`, `die Mietpreisbremse`, `kündigen`) with CEFR tags and IPA phonetics.
  - Grammar schemas (*Wechselpräpositionen*, *Passiv mit Modalverben*).
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"On the backend, Serverpod orchestrates Nebius Token Factory powered by NVIDIA Nemotron. In seconds, Nemotron parses the unstructured prose into a structured Lesson DSL—extracting CEFR-graded lemmas, critical grammar schemas, and an adaptive roleplay scenario tailored to the user's B1 proficiency level."*

---

### Scene 4: Tactical Mini-Games — Kinetic Sentence Builder (1:10 – 1:35)
* **Visual:** User transitions into the 1/4/7 Spaced Priming Queue on the Sanctuary tab, launching the Kinetic Sentence Builder.
* **On-Screen Action:**  
  - Magnetic glass tiles float: `[Ich] [zahle] [die] [Kaution,] [weil] [der] [Mietvertrag] [gültig] [ist.]`.
  - User snaps the tiles into order, demonstrating the German subordinate clause verb-final rule (`weil ... ist`).
  - Haptic feedback trigger & instant green success chime.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Before you jump on a live call, Talkloom primes your active neural pathways through 1/4/7 Ebbinghaus mini-games. The Kinetic Sentence Builder breaks German word-order anxiety with tactile magnetic tiles, training your syntax before you utter a sound."*

---

### Scene 5: Active Voice Arena & Pilot HUD Telemetry (1:35 – 2:25)
* **Visual:** User taps Tab 3 — `🎙️ Arena`. The conversational Voice Arena appears.
* **On-Screen Action:**
  - AI Tutor (Landlord Herr Weber) speaks: *"Guten Tag! Ich habe Ihre Nachricht bezüglich der Kautionsrückzahlung erhalten..."*
  - User replies in German into the mic: *"Ich möchte die Kaution zurückfordern, weil ich die Wohnung fristgerecht kündigen möchte."*
  - **The Magic Moment:** As soon as the user says *"die Kaution"* and *"kündigen"*, the **Spontaneous Production gold badge** pulses and chimes: `Spontaneous production: "die Kaution"!`
  - Pilot HUD Telemetry counters update live:  
    `SPEECH LATENCY: 1.4s` | `COGNITIVE LOAD: 42%` | `PITCH DYNAMICS: 168 Hz`.
  - User toggles the **CEFR Exam Scorecard** displaying breakdown for Listening (88%), Speaking (79%), and Writing (84%).
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Now, the Arena. You converse directly with the pedagogical agent. Behind the scenes, the Pilot HUD measures speech latency, cognitive load, and pitch variance. Crucially, your target objectives are hidden—when you naturally produce 'die Kaution' without being prompted, Talkloom detects spontaneous mastery in real-time."*
